PROJECT SCOPE, PAYMENTS & OWNERSHIP

Service terms

Scope, deliverables, price, and project-specific terms are confirmed in a written agreement before work begins.

Quotes and payments

Initial consultations and quotes are free. Scope and price are agreed in writing before work begins. The default payment structure is 50% deposit after scope approval and 50% before final delivery. Larger projects may use agreed milestones. Payment methods and approved third-party expenses are specified in the agreement; no payments are collected on this website.

Scope and revisions

Base packages include one revision for agreed requirements. New features and changed requirements require a separate quote. Defects that cause a deliverable to fail agreed specifications should be corrected without counting as a discretionary revision.

Founding Client Offer

The first three qualifying paying customers may receive introductory pricing for one eligible project each. Qualification requires written quote acceptance and the required deposit. Discounts cannot be stacked, transferred, or redeemed for cash. Extra work is quoted separately. Availability is confirmed against business records, and the owner may close the offer. Accepted written quotes govern their agreed pricing.

Ownership and licensing

Customers receive ownership or agreed usage rights to final paid deliverables as specified in the project agreement. MWS retains pre-existing tools, reusable libraries, and general methods unless expressly transferred. Third-party components remain subject to their licenses. Ownership and licensing terms must be disclosed before work begins.

Confidentiality and data

Customer data must not be publicly disclosed. Access should be limited to what is necessary for the agreed task. Customer datasets must not be used for model training without explicit authorization. Before data transfer, agree on the secure sharing method, authorized access, retention period, deletion process, and any required recordkeeping exceptions. Do not send sensitive datasets through this website.

Cancellations and refunds

A customer may request cancellation by email. Unstarted work is refundable after deducting only approved, documented nonrecoverable expenses. Completed work is valued against agreed scope or milestones, capped at the approved project price unless additional work was authorized. Payments are credited toward completed work and approved nonrecoverable expenses; unused balances are refunded and any remaining approved balance explained in writing. Deposits are not automatically nonrefundable. The agreement should define calculation and refund timing before work begins.

Limits and project agreements

Data quality, system access, and requirements affect feasibility and pricing. Proofs of concept are not production systems. Specific accuracy, revenue, savings, or business results are not guaranteed. Ongoing support, hosting, integrations, and monitoring require explicit agreement. Project-specific terms are disclosed before work begins and confirmed in the written agreement. Applicable legal rights remain unaffected.
