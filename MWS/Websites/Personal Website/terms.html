Terms of Service

Managed websites, clear pricing, and fair cancellation.

Draft updated October 15, 2026. Have a Louisiana attorney review before use with paying customers.

1. Scope and setup

Starter is one responsive page; Business starts at $349 for up to four simple pages. The first three qualifying Starter projects may receive a $100 introductory setup price; the regular Starter setup price is $130. One consolidated in-scope design revision round is included. Scope and final price are agreed in writing before work begins.

2. Payment and required subscription

A 50% setup deposit is due before work starts. The remaining 50% is due after express approval and before launch. First-month managed care is separately required at launch: $20 Starter or $30 Business. Managed care is required while Maier Web Solutions hosts and manages the site. After the first month, care continues under the customer-authorized monthly ($20/$30) or annual ($200/$300) billing choice until canceled. We will disclose billing dates, payment methods and renewal terms and obtain affirmative consent before charging; no undisclosed automatic charges. Taxes and approved third-party fees, if applicable, are separate.

3. Hosting, domain and ownership

Managed care includes hosting on a suitable platform, periodic site/loading/navigation/contact-link checks, minor technical upkeep and the stated minor-edit allowance. Customer owns their registered domain and controls its renewal. After full setup payment, the customer owns agreed final custom deliverables, subject to third-party licenses and identified pre-existing reusable tools. Subscription cancellation does not take away that ownership. Hosting platform terms and availability may change; uninterrupted service is not guaranteed.

4. Minor edits and fair use

Starter includes up to 1 hour of reasonable minor content updates per monthly service period; Business includes up to 2 hours. Minor edits include changes to existing text, images, contact details, business hours and similar existing content. Time is a maximum allowance, not a guarantee that every requested change is within scope. Time spent reviewing and implementing an approved edit counts toward the allowance; unrelated sales discussions and correcting our own mistakes do not. Unused time does not roll over. Customers should consolidate requests by email. New pages, major redesigns, custom integrations, new functionality and extensive content creation are excluded and may be offered at a separately agreed fixed price. No extra work begins without written approval.

5. Communications and professional conduct

Routine support is handled by email during posted business hours, Monday through Friday excluding holidays. We aim to acknowledge requests within two business days and complete straightforward approved minor edits within five business days after receiving necessary materials; these are targets, not guaranteed emergency response. No 24/7 or immediate service is promised. Repeated calls or messages do not increase priority. Threats, harassment and repeated serious boundary violations may result in refusal of out-of-scope requests or termination after appropriate notice, subject to applicable law and prepaid-fee refunds.

6. Cancellation, transfer and hosting end

Customers may cancel the subscription at any time by emailing us; cancellation stops future renewals after the current paid period. We will confirm receipt and the effective date. Managed hosting normally continues until the end of that paid period and then ends after at least 14 days written notice with export/transfer instructions. Customers may request a reasonable export of their fully paid custom website files and basic handoff instructions, without an extra labor charge for standard handoff. Customer-owned domains are never withheld as leverage. Customers must arrange their own replacement hosting and any third-party services. We do not intentionally delete client-provided content before the stated transfer deadline, subject to legal and provider requirements. Prepaid annual care: unused full months after the effective cancellation date are refunded at 1/12 of the annual price per unused month; partial months are not prorated, subject to law. No cancellation penalty.

7. Project pauses and refunds

If necessary client materials or feedback are more than 14 days late, we may pause after written reminder. After 30 days of inactivity, we may issue a final notice allowing at least 7 days to respond before closing the project. On prelaunch cancellation, documented completed setup work is valued against written milestones, capped at the agreed setup price unless additional work was approved. Payments are credited, overpayments refunded and balances invoiced as applicable. Deposits are not automatically nonrefundable.

8. Quality, responsibility and rate changes

We will use reasonable care and skill. Defects in our original implementation reported within 60 days after launch are corrected without additional labor charges and without reducing the edit allowance, excluding client or third-party changes. We do not guarantee rankings, leads, sales, uptime, or third-party performance. Customers confirm they have rights to supplied materials and approve business claims. Rate changes require at least 60 days written notice, and prepaid annual rates remain fixed through the paid term; customers may cancel before a new rate applies.

9. Disputes and legal terms

Louisiana law generally applies where permitted. Contact us first to attempt informal resolution. Nothing here waives non-waivable consumer rights. Any negotiated liability limitation must be separately drafted and reviewed. The signed client agreement controls if it conflicts with these general terms to the extent legally permitted. This is a draft for legal review, not legal advice.
