One customer.

Why this matters

A cleaner contact list is easier to search and less likely to include repeated messages. This example only removes exact duplicates under the rules you choose. It doesn’t guess missing information.

THE PROBLEM / DIFFERENT SUPPLIER NAMES

A name is only
part of the match.

“Blue Ceramic Mug — 12 oz” and “Mug, Ceramic, Blue, 12oz” look different at first. Compare the details and they could describe the same item. Change the pair to see why a similar name can also be misleading.

Why this matters

This small rule-based example compares type, material, color, and size. Matching attributes suggest a candidate, not a confirmed identity. Brands, identifiers, and specifications still need review. It isn’t a trained machine learning model.
