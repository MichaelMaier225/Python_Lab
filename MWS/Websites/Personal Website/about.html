MY BACKGROUND

Technical work,
with a practical reason.

I’ve worked with product catalogs containing more than 19,000 SKUs, built Python tools to process datasets over 400,000 rows, and worked with Shopify and e-commerce operations.

Working at that scale taught me to pay attention to the structure of the data and the exceptions. A small inconsistency can become a much bigger headache when you have thousands of records.

That’s the approach I bring to MWS: understand what you have, work out what needs to change, and give you something you can actually use.

THE TOOLS BEHIND THE WORK
