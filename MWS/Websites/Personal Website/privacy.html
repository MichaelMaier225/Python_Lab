HOW INQUIRIES & PROJECT DATA ARE HANDLED

Privacy & data policy

This notice explains the website’s contact tools and the data arrangements to agree on before a project starts.

What this website does

The quote form prepares an email locally in your browser. Nothing is submitted to a website backend or saved to browser storage. The email app receives the prepared request only when you choose its link. You must send the email yourself. There are no file uploads or payments on this website.

Information received through contact

If you send an inquiry, MWS receives the name, contact information, business details, and problem description you choose to provide. This information is used to respond, prepare quotes, communicate, and maintain project and business records. Do not include passwords, sensitive personal information, or payment details in an initial inquiry.

Data access and confidentiality

Customer data must not be publicly disclosed. Access should be restricted to what the project needs. Customer datasets must not be used for model training without explicit authorization. Agree on an appropriate secure transfer method and necessary providers before sharing project data.

Providers and website technology

Email and hosting providers may process information needed to deliver their services, including technical access logs such as IP addresses. The primary pages use local assets and no analytics or advertising scripts. Email and hosting providers apply their own policies to the services they provide.

Project retention and deletion

Before project data is transferred, we agree on what is needed, who may access it, how long working copies are retained, and how deletion will be handled. The project agreement should identify provider and backup limitations as well as any records that must be kept for contractual, accounting, or legal reasons. Contact me to discuss a deletion request or the arrangements for a proposed project.

Choices and contact

Contact maierwebsolutions@gmail.com to request correction, access, or deletion of information, subject to necessary legal and recordkeeping obligations. MWS does not sell customer contact information. No internet transmission or storage method is guaranteed completely secure.
