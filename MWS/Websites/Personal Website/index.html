06 / QUESTIONS

A few things
worth knowing.

What do I need to provide to get started?

Tell us about your business, services, and preferred style. You’ll also need to provide the text, photos, logo (if you have one), and contact details you want on the website. We’ll confirm what’s needed before work begins.

How long will my website take?

It depends on the number of pages and how quickly we receive your content and feedback. We’ll agree on a timeline before starting.

What does maintenance actually cover?

Required managed care includes hosting, routine checks and minor updates: up to 1 hour per month on Starter or 2 hours on Business, for reasonable requests. Time does not roll over. New pages and major redesigns require a separate fixed-price quote. Original implementation defects reported within 60 days are corrected without using edit time.

Will I own my website and domain?

After full setup payment, you own the agreed custom website deliverables, subject to third-party licenses and reusable tools. Your domain stays yours. The subscription covers managed hosting and care. You may cancel and request a reasonable export/handoff; our hosting then ends after the paid period and appropriate notice.

How do payments and revisions work?

A 50% setup deposit starts the project, with the remaining 50% after approval and before launch. Required first-month care is charged at launch ($20 Starter or $30 Business). Care continues on the agreed monthly or annual plan until canceled. One in-scope design revision round is included.
