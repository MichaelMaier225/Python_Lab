A FEW THINGS TO KNOW

Questions? Start here.

Do I need to know exactly what I need?

No. Tell us about the repetitive task or confusing spreadsheet. Initial consultations and quotes are free.

Can you work with my Excel or Shopify exports?

Yes. Spreadsheet, CSV, product catalog and Shopify-compatible export workflows are good starting points. Compatibility and scope are confirmed before work begins.

What does “starting at” mean?

The listed prices apply to defined small projects. Data quality, size and complexity affect the final written quote.

How do payments and revisions work?

The default is 50% after scope approval and 50% before final delivery. Base packages include one revision for agreed requirements. Fixing defects against agreed specifications does not use that revision.

Should I send my business data now?

Please describe the problem first. Do not send passwords, payment information, or sensitive datasets. We will agree on an appropriate secure sample-transfer method after initial contact.
