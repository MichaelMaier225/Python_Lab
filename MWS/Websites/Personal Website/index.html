BEFORE YOU REACH OUT

A few fair questions.

Something else on your mind?
Just ask me.

I know what’s annoying me, but not what I need. Is that enough?

Yes. Tell me what you’re doing now and what you wish was easier. You don’t need a technical plan to start the conversation. Initial consultations and quotes are free.

Can you work with Excel and Shopify exports?

Yes. Excel files, CSV exports, and product catalogs are a good place to start. I’ll review the format and confirm what can be done before quoting the work.

Will the project cost exactly the starting price?

Only if it fits the base package. File size, data quality, and the work involved all affect the quote. You’ll know the agreed price before work begins.

How do payment and revisions work?

The default is 50% after scope approval and 50% before final delivery. Base packages include one revision for agreed requirements. Fixing something that fails the agreed specifications doesn’t use up that revision.

Should I email my spreadsheet right away?

Start with a description. Please don’t send passwords, payment information, or sensitive business records. If I need a sample, we’ll agree on an appropriate secure way to share it.
