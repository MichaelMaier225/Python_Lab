Sacratissimum begins with a simple idea: faith in Christ belongs in everyday life. Not only in the places we gather, but in the ways we live, speak, and care for one another.

Our name means “most sacred.” It is a reminder to make space for what has lasting meaning, even in the ordinary moments.

A shared faith. Your own expression.

We are a Christian apparel brand for Christians across denominations. Our focus is clothing that expresses faith thoughtfully, with room for the person wearing it.

This website is a place to discover the collection and learn about each piece. It is a showcase, with no online checkout.

Who is Sacratissimum for?

Christians across denominations who want to express their faith through what they wear. Our shared belief in Christ is at the heart of the brand.

Can I buy a piece here?

This is a collection showcase. You can explore designs, details, sizes and colors; online purchasing is not available. Demo concepts are always labeled.
