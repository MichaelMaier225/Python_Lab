CENTER PERKS RL - TRAINING BASELINE

Extract these files directly into your existing CenterPerksAI folder.
Double-click Run_RL_Training.bat.

This is real tabular Q-learning: exploration, action rewards, Q-value updates,
and saved model. It trains on a simplified prison grid, NOT on the live Steam
game. The saved model does NOT operate The Escapists 1.

To train directly against Steam, a reliable game-state observation and action
interface is still required. Previous screen-based tracking did not provide it.
